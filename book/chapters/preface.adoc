== Preface

This book grows a garden.
You start with a single number, played 48,000 times a second, and end with pieces of music built from parts you understand all the way down.

Every program in the book is real and runs as printed.
Press play next to it to hear it, or open the file it came from and play it yourself:

[source,shell]
----
$ play_program < examples/01-first-sounds/01-tuning-fork.txt
----

WARNING: Turn your speakers or headphones down before the first sound.
Synthesized sound can be much louder than recorded music at the same volume setting.
